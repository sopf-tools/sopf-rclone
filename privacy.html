Privacy Policy

SOPF-Rclone is a personal-use application used to access the owner's Google Drive account through rclone.

Google user data is accessed only for file management operations requested by the account owner.

The application does not sell, share, distribute, or disclose Google user data to third parties.

Data is processed locally by rclone on the owner's computer and through Google Drive APIs.

The application does not support external users.
